# ADR-002: Dual Timestamps & Explicit Timezone Offset Strategy

## Context
Under Peruvian Civil Code Article 1784 and public contracting regulations, quality protocols serve as legal evidence for seven years post-reception. Field devices (Android smartphones) may experience clock drift, intentional clock tampering, or incorrect timezone configurations. Storing a naive timestamp or relying solely on either client or server time creates fatal legal ambiguity during Comptroller audits.

## Decision
Every protocol record preserves two distinct, explicit timestamps:
1. `recorded_at`: The client-reported time of measurement with explicit ISO 8601 timezone offset (e.g., `-05:00` for Peru Time / PET).
2. `server_received_at`: The UTC timestamp generated by the backend upon receipt of the payload.
3. The UI displays project-local time (`America/Lima` / UTC-5).

## Consequences
- Protects the Quality Specialist against accusations of back-dating or retroactively modifying records.
- Enables forensic comparison between capture time and server receipt time to verify offline sync delays.

## Reversibility
**Irreversible**. Database records created without explicit timezone offsets or missing server receipt timestamps cannot be retroactively repaired.
